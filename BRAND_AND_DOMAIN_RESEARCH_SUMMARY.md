## 1. Executive Summary & Product DNA

### 1.1 The Platform
An interactive, browser-based programming education platform designed specifically for Bengali-speaking learners. It departs radically from traditional passive video tutorials.
- **Pedagogical Core Law**: *"গভীরভাবে বোঝো, মুখস্থ নয়"* (Deep conceptual understanding, never rote memorization).
- **Technical Architecture**: In-browser execution powered by Pyodide Web Worker, 14 sequential interactive missions, step-by-step mental models, live terminal output evaluation with fuzzy tolerance, streak/XP gamification.
- **Scope & Future Roadmap**: Currently focused on Python, but engineered to be completely **language-agnostic** (expanding into JavaScript, Data Structures, Algorithms, SQL, and System Design).

### 1.2 The Founder's Constraints & Budget
- **Budget Profile**: Student founder on a strict budget.
- **Purchasing Rule**: Can afford only **ONE single `.com` domain** (~$9.40 to ~$10.40/year via Spaceship or Porkbun with free WHOIS privacy).
- **Zero Defensive Buying**: Cannot afford purchasing multiple TLDs (`.org`, `.net`, `.io`) or protective alternative spellings. Therefore, the chosen name must be **100% acoustically distinct and bulletproof**.

## 2. Naming Criteria & Forensic Validation Rules

Any recommended candidate brand name must strictly pass all 5 validation filters:

1. **The "Radio Test" (100% Acoustic Precision)**:
   - When spoken aloud on a podcast, YouTube video, or conversation, there must be only **ONE obvious spelling**.
   - Zero "C vs K" traps, zero "S vs SH" ambiguities, zero double-vowel dilemmas (`ee/i` or `oo/u`), and zero silent letters.
2. **Exact `.com` Verisign RDAP Availability (HTTP 404)**:
   - Must be unregistered on the authoritative Verisign registry (`https://rdap.verisign.com/com/v1/domain/<name>.com` returning HTTP 404).
   - Must be available at standard retail registration price (~$9.40/year), not held by aftermarket domain brokers or squatted as a premium domain.
3. **Clean-Slate Google SERP Footprint (Zero Conflict)**:
   - No multi-million dollar tech giants, NASDAQ/publicly-traded companies, active trademarks, or conflicting software products dominating Google Page 1.
   - Clean slate allows the new platform to rank #1 on Google for its brand keyword within weeks of launch.
4. **Invented Portmanteau / Neologism Style**:
   - Inspired by modern Silicon Valley tech giants (e.g., *Duolingo, Coursera, Vercel, Supabase, Figma, Spotify, Codecrafters, Replit*).
   - Combines cognitive depth, logic, and craftsmanship into a single memorable word.
5. **Language-Agnostic & Scalable**:
   - Must not contain `py` or `python` so that future non-Python courses (JavaScript, Go, Algorithms) fit naturally without rebranding.

  *Syntax + Forge = Syntaforge* (The interactive coding workshop where code syntax and developer intuition are forged hands-on).

### Archetype 4: "The Electric Neologism" Model (Vercel & Figma Archetype)
